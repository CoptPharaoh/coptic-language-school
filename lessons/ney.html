Ney

Learn the Coptic letter Ney: Ⲛ ⲛ.

Practice Together

Ⲛ ⲛ

Ney makes the sound “N.”

Follow Along

  1. Point to the letter: Ⲛ ⲛ
  2. Say “Ney” out loud.
  3. Say the sound “N.”
  4. Trace the letter on the worksheet.
  5. Color the letter Ney.

Lesson Goals

  • Recognize Ney.
  • Hear the sound clearly.
  • Repeat after the lesson.
  • Practice tracing and coloring.
